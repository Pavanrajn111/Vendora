Platform Capabilities

Built For Seamless Multi-Vendor Commerce

Vendora eliminates friction for both shoppers and sellers with automated workflows, real-time tracking, and simulated payment confirmation.

Verified Storefront Discovery

Customers can explore authentic merchant catalogues, view seller credentials, and connect directly to their favorite vendors with a single click.

Real-Time Inventory Control

Vendors manage listings with automated image resolvers, multi-extension asset detection, and live availability indicators (In Stock / Out of Stock).

Simulated Dual Payment Gateway

Complete realistic checkout using dynamic UPI QR generation (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), manual UPI IDs, or Luhn-validated test cards.

End-to-End Delivery Timeline

Track full lifecycle transitions: from Pending to Accepted, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered with expected arrival dates.

Authentic Rating & Review System

Shoppers leave verified ratings and written feedback. Vendors monitor customer satisfaction to foster platform reputation.

Defense-in-Depth Security

CSRF token validation on all forms, login rate limiting, parameterized SQLite queries, Pillow upload validation, and strict multi-tenant isolation.

Simple & Transparent

How Vendora Works

Designed for zero-confusion order execution between customers and vendors.

1

Connect to Vendors

Browse active stores, explore verified catalogues, and establish direct merchant connections with one tap.

2

Place Order & Pay

Select in-stock items. Once the vendor accepts, complete simulated checkout via Dynamic UPI QR or test card.

3

Track & Review

Follow shipment updates through out-for-delivery milestones, receive your order, and publish honest merchant ratings.
